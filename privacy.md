# Privacy Policy

**Taqwim — Islamic Calendar**
**Last Updated: March 8, 2026**

## Overview

Taqwim is built with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Taqwim does not collect, share, or sell any personal information. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party data collection of any kind.

## Device Permissions

Taqwim may request the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

### Location

Taqwim requests access to your location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Your location data is used on-device only, is never transmitted to any server, and is not stored beyond a temporary 5-minute cache used to minimize repeated location requests during a single session.

### Calendar

Taqwim requests access to your device calendar only if you enable the Calendar Sync feature. This allows the app to create a dedicated "Taqwim" calendar and add Islamic events to it. No existing calendar data is read, modified, or transmitted.

### Notifications

Taqwim may request permission to send local notifications for Islamic event reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve any external servers.

## Third-Party Services

Taqwim does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. Prayer time calculations are performed entirely on-device using the open-source Adhan library.

## Data Storage

All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. No data is synced to the cloud or transmitted externally.

## Children's Privacy

Taqwim does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
