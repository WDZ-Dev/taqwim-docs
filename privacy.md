# Privacy Policy

**Taqwim — Islamic Calendar (iOS and Android)**
**Last Updated: November 4, 2026**

## Overview

Taqwim is built with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Taqwim does not collect, share, or sell any personal information. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party data collection of any kind.

## Device Permissions

Taqwim may request the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

### Location

Taqwim requests access to your location solely to calculate accurate prayer times and sunset for your area. On Android you can choose precise or approximate location. Your location is used on-device only and is never sent to us or any third party. The last known location is stored on your device so the home screen widgets can show prayer times.

To display your city name, the app uses your device's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android), which may send your coordinates to that provider under its own privacy policy. Taqwim itself never receives or stores this data off-device.

### Calendar

Taqwim requests access to your device calendar only if you enable the Calendar Sync feature. This allows the app to create a dedicated "Taqwim" calendar and add Islamic events to it. Your existing calendars and events are not read, modified, or transmitted.

### Notifications and Alarms

Taqwim may request permission to send local notifications for Islamic event reminders. On Android, it may also ask to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive on time. These are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve any external servers.

## Third-Party Services

Taqwim does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. Prayer time calculations are performed entirely on-device using the open-source Adhan library.

## Data Storage

All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. Taqwim does not sync or transmit them to any server. On Android, app data is excluded from cloud backup. On iOS, app data may be included in your own device backup under Apple's standard backup settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Taqwim does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
