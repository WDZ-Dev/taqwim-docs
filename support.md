# Support

**Taqwim — Islamic Calendar**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The Hijri date doesn't match my local mosque. How do I fix it?

Different regions may observe slightly different Hijri dates based on local moon sighting. Go to **Settings > Calendar Settings** and use the **Hijri Adjustment** slider to shift the date by up to ±2 days to match your local community.

### Which calculation method should I use?

- **Umm al-Qura** — Used in Saudi Arabia and widely adopted. This is the default.
- **Islamic Civil** — A fixed arithmetic calendar commonly used for civil purposes.
- **Islamic Tabular** — A rule-based arithmetic calendar used in some regions.

If you are unsure, Umm al-Qura is recommended.

### My prayer times seem incorrect. What should I do?

1. Make sure you have granted Taqwim location access in **iOS Settings > Taqwim > Location**.
2. Check that the correct **Prayer Method** is selected in **Settings > Prayer Times Settings**. Choose the method used by your local authority or mosque.
3. Verify the correct **Madhab** is selected — Hanafi calculates a later Asr time than Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali.

### Why does the date change in the evening?

In the Islamic calendar, the new day begins at sunset (Maghrib). Taqwim reflects this by advancing the date after Maghrib prayer time.

### How do I sync Islamic events to my iPhone calendar?

Go to **Settings > Display Settings** and enable **Calendar Sync**. Taqwim will create a dedicated "Taqwim" calendar on your device and add Islamic events to it.

### What are the colored dots on the calendar?

Each dot represents an event on that day. The colors indicate the event type:
- **Gold** — Eid or Hajj
- **Purple** — Ramadan
- **Green** — Holiday
- **Blue** — Recommended fasting day
- **Copper** — Historical event

### What are sunnah fasting days?

These are days on which fasting is recommended (but not obligatory) in Islam. Taqwim highlights Monday & Thursday fasts, Ayyam al-Beed (13th–15th of each month), the Day of Arafah, and the Six Days of Shawwal. You can toggle these on or off in **Settings > Display Settings**.
